# Boxing asset provenance

1. Compact gloves and pictogram boxer: original procedural geometry authored in `Assets/Boxing/Editor/BoxingContent.cs`; ring and gym in `BoxingProject.cs`. No downloaded character, texture or animation assets. Unity built-in primitive meshes are construction primitives. Hapbeat-authored content uses the repository MIT license.
2. Haptic clips `Assets/Boxing/Haptics/stream-clips/<cue>.wav` (defaults until authored in Hapbeat Studio): the `*_soft` clips are copies of the former `BodySoft.wav`, an original deterministic 75 Hz synthesis (16 kHz PCM16 mono, MIT); the `*_hard` clips are copies of the former `damage.wav`, the Hapbeat-adjusted damage clip (added in commit 9e4315e). These tactile clips are independent of the audible recordings.
3. Unity Engine, Universal Render Pipeline, XR Hands, OpenXR, XR Interaction Toolkit and Unity UI: development/runtime packages resolved by Unity Package Manager; their bundled licenses apply. They are not represented as CC0 assets. Unity's built-in `LegacyRuntime.ttf` is used for UI text.
4. Hapbeat SDK and Demo Switch: Hapbeat source code; respective repository/package licenses apply.
5. Editor-only XR Interaction Simulator: the core prefab, input actions and hand-expression data are imported from Unity XR Interaction Toolkit **3.3.1**, `Samples~/XR Interaction Simulator`. Unity's bundled package license applies (not CC0; copyright notice preserved in `Assets/Boxing/Editor/Simulator/LICENSE.md`). The sample UI and its art/scripts are not imported; UI/icon references were removed, and automatic removal of other HMD devices is disabled. Stale references to the classic hand-control asset were redirected to the included interaction hand-control asset with identical Action IDs. These utilities are isolated under `Assets/Boxing/Editor/Simulator`; player build reports verify they are not packaged in the game.

6. Menu ghost hands: this repository ships original placeholder hands (`Assets/Boxing/Art/PlaceholderHands/`), built from Unity built-in sphere/capsule meshes by `Assets/Boxing/Editor/BoxingPlaceholderHands.cs` (MIT). Their joint names follow the Unity XR Hands convention so `XRHandSkeletonDriver` can drive them. The Unity XR Hands sample hand meshes (`LeftHand.fbx` / `RightHand.fbx`, Unity Package Distribution License, notice in `Assets/Boxing/Art/UnityHands/LICENSE.md`) are **not** included in this repository because that license does not allow redistribution as source assets. Builds made by Hapbeat with the separately held meshes linked under `Assets/HapbeatPrivate/` contain them only as compiled binary data; builds from a plain clone contain only the placeholder hands.

7. Audible impacts: Kenney, [Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Downloaded 2026-09-14 from the official pack. `Assets/Boxing/Haptics/sounds/impactSoft_medium_000.wav` and `impactSoft_heavy_000.wav` are the default glove-contact sounds, `impactPunch_medium_000.wav` and `impactPunch_heavy_000.wav` the default body-contact sounds: the pack's OGG files decoded to 16-bit PCM WAV (44.1 kHz stereo), otherwise unchanged. Pack License.txt identifies Kenney and CC0; commercial use, modification and redistribution are allowed, attribution is not required.
8. Boxing bell: Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, [Boxing bell #1, sound 1926](https://bigsoundbank.com/boxing-bell-1-s1926.html), released as CC0 on that page. Downloaded 2026-09-14 from `https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/bwf-en/1926.wav`. `Assets/Boxing/Audio/BoxingBellSource.wav` retains the recording; `BoxingGongFour.wav` is an edited four-strike sequence (0, 0.24, 0.48, 0.88 seconds) built from the final strike, with mono mixdown, peak normalization and tail fade. The same edited clip plays at round start and end. No endorsement implied.
9. Round music: Umplix, [Boxing Ring](https://opengameart.org/content/boxing-ring-0) (OpenGameArt), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); credit is optional. `Assets/Boxing/Audio/Music/BoxingRingMusic.wav` is the music `boxing_ring_-_music.wav` with the crowd track `boxing_match_audience.wav` from the same page (also Umplix, CC0) mixed in: the crowd resampled to 44.1 kHz, looped with a 2 s crossfade and mixed 2 dB lower; peak -1 dBFS, 54.985 s, 16-bit stereo, RIFF metadata chunks removed. License notes: `Assets/Boxing/Audio/Music/License.txt`.
10. Tutorial narration: `Assets/Boxing/Audio/Tutorial/*.wav`, synthesized from `Voice/voice-lines.json` with the local [AivisSpeech Engine](https://github.com/Aivis-Project/AivisSpeech-Engine) and its bundled voice model まお (Mao), under the [Aivis Common Model License 1.0](https://github.com/Aivis-Project/ACML/blob/master/ACML-1.0.md): commercial use is allowed and credit is optional. Normalized to -20 LUFS with ffmpeg (hapbeat-demos `tools/tts`).

The project's CC0 requirement applies to externally sourced game visual/audio content. The impact, bell and music audio above is CC0, and the narration voice (10) is under ACML 1.0 as stated; code/original geometry remain MIT and Unity-licensed engine primitives/tooling retain their own licenses. No Ultimate Glove Ball/Meta game art or Unity XRI decorative sample art is included.
